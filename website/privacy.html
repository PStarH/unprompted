Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This notice describes this website and the open-source Unprompted application. The application runs on your device; the website and connected services have separate data flows.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. Its processing, retention, international transfers and privacy contact are described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links take you to services with their own policies.

The site does not include project-operated analytics, advertising trackers, account registration or forms. Its fonts, images and scripts are served from the site. The install-command button writes to your clipboard when pressed. The landing page keeps its animation and language preferences in memory for the current page.

The local application

Unprompted runs a server on your machine and stores a local workspace. Depending on the version and connections you enable, it can store synchronized email and document content, selected local files, task context, evidence, drafts, history, memories and connection credentials. It uses this information to discover and organize work, prepare actions you request and support the application’s user-facing features. The current application has no project-hosted application backend or analytics endpoint.

The server binds to localhost and has no built-in authentication. Do not expose it to a network. Local workspace data, saved tokens and model keys are not encrypted at rest by the application. File permissions and device-level encryption can help protect your computer, but they are not a guarantee against unauthorized access.

Google account data

If you connect Google and grant the requested permissions, the application can access the account information and Gmail, Google Drive or document data needed by the features you select, including message content and metadata, file names and document content. Depending on your version, selected features and additional permissions, it may also access other Google Workspace data. It uses this information locally to identify work, present supporting context and prepare or carry out actions you authorize, such as drafts, messages or document changes. The permissions requested and actions available depend on your selections and the installed version.

Google content and derived records may be saved in the local workspace. If you configure a remote model, selected context, including Google content, may be sent to that model provider to perform the requested task. An action you authorize can also send content to its chosen destination. Those recipients handle data under their own policies; check their retention and model-training terms before connecting them. A compatible local model can avoid sending context to a remote model endpoint.

Google API data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy and, for applicable Workspace data, its Limited Use requirements. These requirements restrict transfers and the use of Google data for advertising or training generalized AI models.

Other connections and permissions

Reading a connected cloud source contacts that provider; using a configured model or tool contacts its endpoint. Connect only sources you are authorized to use. External actions depend on the permissions you grant and the controls in your version; review the destination, evidence and scope before authorizing them. Your organization and the providers you choose may have their own privacy and security requirements.

Retention, deletion and your controls

Local records can remain on your device until you clear or delete them. Depending on your version, you can pause observation, disconnect a source, clear its local copy or use Forget for selected records. A disconnect alone can retain local data. Review any cleanup status and the privacy documentation; cleanup can be limited by active operations, database files, migration backups or independent copies. You can also remove stored workspace and configuration files using the storage documentation, after stopping the application and preserving anything you need.

To remove the application’s Google access, also review and revoke it in your Google Account connections. Removing local data does not delete the original items in Google or another source, data already sent to a model or other provider, or copies outside the app. Contact those providers about their copies and retention.

Questions and privacy requests

For a privacy question or request, contact the project maintainer through the private contact methods, if available, on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. You may also open a minimal GitHub issue requesting a private channel. Issues and pull requests are public: do not post credentials, workspace files, private messages or sensitive data. Follow the security reporting instructions for vulnerabilities.

Depending on your location and who controls the relevant processing, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal data, object to or restrict processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact GitHub, your organization or a connected provider about data they control; the maintainer cannot directly erase their copies.

Changes

The date above identifies this notice. Repository history records revisions. Material changes in data use may require further notice or consent under applicable law or provider policies.
